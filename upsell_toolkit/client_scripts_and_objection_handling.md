# THE UPSELL BATTLECARD & CLIENT SCRIPTS
## Strategy & Objection Handling Playbook for Naïa Pilates Studio
**Target:** Upselling the client from a "Logo-Only" purchase ($850) to the "Studio Identity System" ($2,400) or "Turnkey Atelier" ($4,600).

---

## 1. The Psychology of the Wellness Studio Client

Why did the client insist they "only want a logo"?
1. **Fear of Scope Creep & Budget Overrun**: They are likely facing heavy startup expenses (reformer equipment leases, studio buildout, commercial deposits).
2. **Lack of Category Awareness**: They confuse a *logo* with a *brand identity*. They believe a logo file is all a printer needs to make everything look good.
3. **Overwhelm**: Opening a studio requires hundreds of decisions; they want to tick off the "logo box" as fast as possible.

### The Golden Rule of This Upsell:
> **Never argue with the client. Validate their logo choice enthusiastically, and then show them that the magic they just fell in love with is actually the system, not just the isolated vector.**

## 3. Objection Handling Battlecard

### Objection 1: "I really only have the budget for the logo right now."
**The Response:**
> "I completely understand. Opening a studio comes with substantial upfront capital, from equipment leases to deposits. That's actually why getting the identity system right now saves you thousands in the next 60 days.  
>  
> For example: Every client who attends a reformer class must wear non-slip grip socks for safety. If you sell unbranded generic socks, you make maybe $3 a pair. With custom Naïa grip socks (which we design in the Studio Kit), studios sell them for $22 at a $15+ margin. Selling just 150 pairs of socks pays for the entire difference of the identity system.  
>  
> What if we split the Studio Kit into 2 convenient milestone payments of $1,200? That way, you don't feel the cash flow impact today, but your studio opens with the exact high-ticket aesthetic you want."

---

### Objection 2: "Can't my sign maker or website builder just pick the fonts and colors?"
**The Response:**
> "Sign makers and web coders are great at fabrication and programming, but they are not brand strategists. A sign maker will typically use default Arial or whatever free font is installed on their cutting plotter. If they pick the wrong gold foil or wrong letter spacing, you’ll look at your storefront every morning wishing it matched the render.  
>  
> With the Studio Kit, I provide the exact vector production blueprints, scale measurements, and material specifications for your sign company. You literally just hand them the file and say, 'Produce this.' You save dozens of back-and-forth revisions and prevent costly reprinting mistakes."

---

### Objection 3: "I can just create my own Instagram graphics in Canva."
**The Response:**
> "You definitely can, and in fact, that's exactly how we set you up to succeed! In the Studio Identity System, I don't just give you static images; I build you 12 custom, branded Canva templates using your exact fonts, colors, and layout grids.  
>  
> That way, when you or your front-desk team want to post a new instructor spotlight, a weekend schedule, or a member testimonial, you just type in the text and hit post—and every single post looks like a high-fashion wellness magazine instead of a DIY graphic."

---

### Objection 4: "Can we start with the logo now and do the rest in 3 or 4 months?"
**The Response (The "Bridging Deposit" Technique):**
> "You can, but here is what happens in practice: During pre-launch is when you build your founding member waitlist, print your window graphics, order your retail inventory, and launch your Instagram. If your pre-launch looks disjointed, you miss the initial opening wave.  
>  
> Here is what I can do: Let's lock in the Logo baseline today ($850). If you decide within the next 7 days to upgrade to the Studio Identity System, I will credit 100% of your $850 toward the package and lock in the bundled package rate of $2,400 (which saves you $600 compared to booking the items piecemeal later). Does that feel like a fair way to get started?"
